# Quality rubric

Score each dimension from 0 to 4 using the anchors. Total out of 32.

- **Pass**: 26 or more, with no dimension below 3 and dimension 2 (traceability) at 4.
- Below that, fix the lowest-scoring dimensions and score again.

When auditing someone else's synthesis, report the score per dimension with one specific
example for each score below 3.

| # | Dimension | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Question and scope** | No clear question; scope undefined | Question stated; criteria partial or set after reading | Answerable question tied to a decision; criteria fixed in advance; deviations recorded |
| 2 | **Traceability** | Claims uncited, or citations that cannot be found | Most claims cited; some numbers or quotations without locators | Every claim, number and quotation traces to a located passage in a source that was read; all references verified |
| 3 | **Source handling** | Sources taken at face value | Some appraisal; lineage or access not considered | Every source appraised for this question; lineages assigned; access levels recorded; exclusions reasoned |
| 4 | **Synthesis** | Source-by-source summary | Grouped by topic; themes are labels | Organised by claims that could be wrong, each drawing on several sources and saying more than any one of them |
| 5 | **Disagreement and gaps** | One-sided; no contrary evidence | Conflicts listed but unexplained; gaps mentioned in passing | Contrary evidence sought and reported; conflicts explained or marked unexplained; gaps in evidence and limits of the synthesis stated |
| 6 | **Calibration** | Uniformly confident or uniformly hedged | Ratings given but wording does not follow them, or rationale missing | Each claim rated with a specific rationale; wording matches rating; nothing rated above its ceiling |
| 7 | **Decision value** | Reader cannot tell what follows | Implications present but generic | Bottom line first; implications tied to the decision, with options, conditions and what would change the conclusion; recommendations separated from findings |
| 8 | **Method transparency** | No account of method | Method described loosely | Review type named honestly; search, counts, criteria, appraisal and synthesis method stated; assumptions listed |

## Quick checks before scoring

- Pick the three claims the bottom line most depends on. Open each cited source at its
  locator. Does it say that?
- Read only the bottom line and the finding headings. Is the answer clear from those alone?
- Search the text for "studies show", "research suggests", "experts", "many", "most".
  Is each one cited and counted?
- Is there at least one place where the report says the evidence is weak, absent or
  against the main conclusion?
- Could someone repeat the search from the method section?
