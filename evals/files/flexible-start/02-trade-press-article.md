# "Flexible starts slash staff turnover by a quarter" (fictitious)

Warehouse Weekly, 14 April 2023

A year-long pilot at Northfield Logistics has shown that letting staff choose their start
time cuts turnover by almost a quarter. Turnover fell to 13% in depots offering flexible
starts, compared with 17% elsewhere. "This proves flexibility is the answer to the
retention crisis," said Northfield's HR director. Experts agree the findings will change
how the sector thinks about shifts.
