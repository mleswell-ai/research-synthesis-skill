# Shift flexibility and intention to leave: a cross-sectional survey (fictitious)

**Abstract only. The full paper was not available.**

Journal of Applied Workforce Studies, 2021.

We surveyed 2,310 warehouse and distribution staff across 41 employers (response rate
22%). Staff who reported having some control over their start time were less likely to
say they intended to leave within a year (odds ratio 0.71, 95% CI 0.58 to 0.87), after
adjusting for age, tenure and pay band. Flexible scheduling may be a promising retention
strategy.
