# Northfield Logistics: Flexible Start Pilot, Final Report (fictitious)

Prepared by the Northfield People Analytics team, March 2023.

## 1. Design

Twelve depots were randomly assigned: six offered flexible start times (any start between
06:00 and 10:00) for twelve months, six continued with fixed 08:00 starts. Assignment was
by coin toss at depot level. 1,184 staff were in post at baseline (flexible 602, fixed 582).

## 2. Results

Table 2. Voluntary turnover over twelve months

| Arm | Staff at baseline | Voluntary leavers | Turnover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flexible start | 602 | 78 | 13.0% |
| Fixed start | 582 | 99 | 17.0% |

Difference: 4.0 percentage points (95% CI 0.0 to 8.1), p = 0.05.

Table 3. Turnover by tenure (page 9)

| Tenure at baseline | Flexible | Fixed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Under 1 year | 21.4% | 31.9% |
| 1 year or more | 9.8% | 10.6% |

The tenure analysis was not planned in advance.

## 3. Limitations (page 11)

Two flexible depots opened new canteens during the pilot. Depot managers knew their
allocation. Twelve depots is a small number of clusters, and the analysis did not adjust
for clustering.
